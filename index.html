      📜 Game Rules
      🎮 Basic Rules
      01
      Both players pick a seat to join — game starts automatically.
      02
      The player whose turn it is chooses Truth or Dare.
      03
      The other player types a question (truth) or a challenge (dare).
      04
      The first player submits their answer — then the turn switches.
      ✦ Truth Rules Truth
      T1
      You must tell the truth — your partner will know if you're lying!
      T2
      Questions can get personal, but always keep it respectful.
      T3
      Skipping triggers an automatic punishment spin!
      🔥 Dare Rules Dare
      D1
      You must complete the dare — send proof (screenshot, selfie, etc.).
      D2
      You have a 5-minute time limit to complete the dare.
      D3
      Passing triggers a punishment spin — no mercy!
      ⚡ Punishment Rules Punishment
      P1
      Punishments cannot be refused — final is final.
      P2
      Punishments are randomly spun (unless Opponent Chooses lands).
      P3
      You must send proof of completing your punishment.
